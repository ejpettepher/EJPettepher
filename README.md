### About

Howdy y'all — I go by Ethan or EJ. I'm an applied infectious disease and environmental epidemiologist (MPH, Emory RSPH '25; CPH, NBPHE '26) interested in diving deeper into infectious disease dynamics, healthcare system operations, and global health and biosecurity. Specifically, I'm interested in testing how we can use complex systems thinking, mechanistic & deterministic modeling, and spatio-temporal & network analytics to better understand and mitigate future risk in these areas. This space is 10% professional portfolio and 90% digital workbench for sharpening my data analysis and programming skills, so please excuse the mess!
